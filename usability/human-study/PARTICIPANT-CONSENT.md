# Participant information and consent checklist

Use this as a plain-language session checklist. It is not legal advice or a substitute for any organizational research/privacy review that applies.

## Information to explain

- The session is to improve WJA Bid. We are evaluating the app, not the participant.
- Participation is voluntary. The participant can pause, skip a question, or stop at any time without giving a reason.
- The session should use a synthetic or redacted bid PDF. The participant should avoid showing personal, company-confidential, or operationally sensitive material.
- Notes will describe actions and feedback without the participant's name or employee number. Explain who can access the notes and how long they will be kept.
- Recording is optional. Explain what is recorded, who can access it, how it will be used, and the specific deletion date. A participant may take part without being recorded.
- Explain whether any screenshots will be captured and ensure they do not reveal personal schedules, account details, or notifications.
- No findings will be described as proof that all pilots behave the same way.

## Ask and record only yes/no

- I understand what this session is for and that I can stop at any time: **Yes / No**
- I agree to take part: **Yes / No**
- I agree to audio/video/screen recording (optional): **Yes / No / Not requested**
- I agree to de-identified notes being used to improve the app: **Yes / No**
- The moderator has explained the recording deletion date, if applicable: **Yes / Not applicable**

If participation consent is “No,” do not begin. If recording consent is “No,” continue without recording if the participant still agrees to take part. Do not store this form with names unless the owner has an approved, secure process for it.
